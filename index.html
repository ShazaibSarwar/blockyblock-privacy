BlockyBlock Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the BlockyBlock mobile game ("the app"). It is published by Shazaib Sarwar ("we", "us"). You can contact us at info.shasar@gmail.com.

Short version

BlockyBlock works offline, has no accounts, and we do not run any servers that collect your data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising and billing services collect, described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, best score and settings (sound, haptics, color theme, block style, block patterns, and whether ads were removed) on your device so the game can resume. This information stays on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

We do not receive this data and we do not sell your personal information. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Your choices. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. You can review your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where applicable). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Purchases

You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We only learn that the purchase of "Remove ads" was completed so we can turn ads off for you. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information, except that Google receives data through its advertising and billing services as described above.

Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
